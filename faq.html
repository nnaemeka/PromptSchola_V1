FAQ

Answers that keep you moving.

PromptSchola is built for final-year high school and first-year university students who want a guided way to learn with AI—without guessing what to ask next.

What is PromptSchola?
A STEM learning site that turns each topic into a guided 6-step nano-lesson (Orient → Understand → Apply → Work Through It → Review & Practice → Simulation Lab).
What do I get for free?
Everyone can view and copy Steps 1–3 prompts. Signed-in free users can run AI for Steps 1–2, with a daily limit.
What does Mastery unlock?
Run AI for Steps 1–6 and full Help access (including help pages). Also removes daily usage limits.
Why is Help locked on free?
Help includes prompt scaffolds and deeper guidance. Locking it keeps the free tier usable while protecting the system’s “guided mastery” value.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Use the “Manage billing” button on Pricing to open the Stripe Customer Portal. If you cancel, access downgrades immediately.
