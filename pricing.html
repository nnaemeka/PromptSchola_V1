FAQ

Do I get instant access?

Yes. After Stripe confirms your checkout, your account is upgraded automatically (via webhook), and full mastery unlocks.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel and keep access through the end of your current billing period. (Optional: we can add the Stripe Customer Portal button next.)

Why is Help locked on free?

Help + AI calls have real API costs. Locking Help and advanced steps keeps PromptSchola sustainable, while still letting free users learn the system and start strong.
