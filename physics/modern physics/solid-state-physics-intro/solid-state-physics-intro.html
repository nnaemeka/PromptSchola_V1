Classification
Conductors, insulators, semiconductors
The band gap size (and band occupancy) separates conductors, insulators, and semiconductors.
  • Conductors: accessible states for electrons
  • Insulators: large gap blocks conduction
  • Semiconductors: small gap, tunable behavior
