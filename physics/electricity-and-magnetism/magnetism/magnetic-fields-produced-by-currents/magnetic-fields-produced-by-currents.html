Magnetic Fields Produced by Currents

Before Biot–Savart and Ampère’s law, build intuition for field shapes and symmetry.

Geometry
Magnetic field around straight wires
A long straight wire produces circular magnetic field lines centered on the wire. The direction follows a right-hand grip rule.
  • Field lines are concentric circles
  • Direction from current direction
  • Strength decreases with distance
  • Cylindrical symmetry is the key

Loops
Magnetic field of loops
A current loop produces a field resembling a bar magnet: field lines pass through the loop and return outside, forming closed loops.
  • Field strongest near the center
  • Direction from right-hand rule
  • Loop behaves like a magnetic dipole
  • Geometry guides qualitative reasoning

Devices
Solenoids and toroids
Many closely spaced loops form coils. A solenoid produces an approximately uniform field inside, while a toroid confines the field to the core region.
  • Solenoid: nearly uniform interior field
  • Toroid: field largely confined inside
  • Field direction from winding direction
  • Symmetry simplifies analysis
